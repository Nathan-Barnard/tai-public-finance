# Literature

Reference material used in developing this project. The PDFs themselves are
gitignored (most are commercially published and shouldn't be redistributed
from a public repo) — this file tracks what's here so the bibliography is
still visible without the files.

## Textbooks

- Fleming, W. H. & Soner, H. M. — *Controlled Markov Processes and Viscosity
  Solutions* (2nd ed., Springer)
- Øksendal, B. & Sulem, A. — *Applied Stochastic Control of Jump Diffusions*
  (3rd ed., Springer)
- Acemoglu, D. — *Introduction to Modern Economic Growth* — **partial: Parts
  5–9 only**
- Ljungqvist, L. & Sargent, T. J. — *Recursive Macroeconomic Theory* (2018 ed.)
- Scheidegger, S. — *Deep Learning for Solving and Estimating Dynamic Models
  in Economics and Finance* (July 2026). Text/figures CC0, code MIT —
  openly licensed. Companion code:
  <https://github.com/sischei/Deep_Learning_for_Solving_And_Estimating_Dynamic_Economic_Models>

## Lecture notes

- Brunnermeier, M. K., Merkel, S. & Sannikov, Y. — *Lectures on Macro, Money,
  and Finance: A Heterogeneous-Agent Continuous-Time Approach* (Princeton,
  ECO 529)

## Papers

- Acemoglu, D. & Restrepo, P. (2018) — "The Race between Man and Machine:
  Implications of Technology for Growth, Factor Shares, and Employment,"
  *American Economic Review* 108(6): 1488–1542.
- Acemoglu, D. & Restrepo, P. (2018) — "Artificial Intelligence, Automation
  and Work," NBER Working Paper 24196.
- Acemoglu, D., Manera, A. & Restrepo, P. (2020) — "Does the US Tax Code
  Favor Automation?," NBER Working Paper 27052.
- Moll, B., Rachel, L. & Restrepo, P. (2022) — "Uneven Growth: Automation's
  Impact on Income and Wealth Inequality," *Econometrica* 90(6): 2645–2683.
  Plus online appendix.
